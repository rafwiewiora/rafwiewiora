### SAR Brain

In SAR Brain, you replay a published medicinal chemistry campaign and choose which compound to predict, make, or test next. Each choice changes what you know and what the next experiment costs.

**[▶ Play the demo](https://rafwiewiora.github.io/sar-brain/strategy_movie.html#RENIN)** · [Read the interactive preprint](https://rafwiewiora.github.io/sar-brain/preprint.html)

### Other projects

- **[Molarium](https://molarium.org/)** is an open-source workbench for designing molecules and running molecular simulations in the browser.
- **[Foldarium](https://github.com/rafwiewiora/foldarium)** lets chemists compare predicted protein–ligand binding poses and record which ones they trust. Those choices help us study where human judgment adds to model confidence scores.
