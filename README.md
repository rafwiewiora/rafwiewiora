### SAR Brain

SAR Brain is a reinforcement learning environment for medicinal chemistry. Replay a published campaign and try to find its most potent compound in as few synthesis steps as possible. Each assay gives you a new reference for predictions. Each intermediate you make can lower the cost of making related compounds.

### Other projects

- **[Molarium](https://molarium.org/)** is an open-source workbench for designing molecules and running molecular simulations in the browser.
- **[Foldarium](https://github.com/rafwiewiora/foldarium)** lets chemists compare predicted protein–ligand binding poses and record which ones they trust. Those choices help us study where human judgment adds to model confidence scores.
